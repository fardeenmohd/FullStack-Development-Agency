- Implement a real-time lead scoring dashboard that provides visual insights into the performance of AI-generated leads based on various metrics such as confidence score, conversion rate, and compliance risk. This dashboard should allow users to filter and sort leads by these metrics for better decision-making.
- Add a feature that allows exporters to set up automated notifications for lead status changes, such as when a lead is contacted or converted into a transaction. This will help exporters stay informed and respond promptly to opportunities.
- Introduce a 'Lead Pipeline' feature that visualizes the entire journey of leads from discovery to conversion into transactions. This feature should include a timeline view showing key milestones such as lead scoring, notification triggers, and transaction initiation. It will provide a comprehensive overview of the sales process and help exporters identify bottlenecks or areas for improvement.
- Introduce a 'Product Catalog Management' feature that allows exporters to easily update and manage their product listings in the system. This feature should include functionalities such as uploading new products with HS codes, editing existing product details, and deleting unused products. It will enhance the efficiency of product management and ensure that all listed products are up-to-date and accurate.
